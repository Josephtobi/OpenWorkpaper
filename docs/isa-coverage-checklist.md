# ISA Coverage Checklist (MVP)

Use this checklist during template QA before release.

## Universal Coverage
- ISA 200 series quality, ethics, and engagement acceptance coverage exists in planning templates.
- ISA 300/315/330 planning-risk-response chain is represented as required questions.
- ISA 240 fraud procedures and management override tests are mapped.
- ISA 520 analytical procedures appear in planning and completion phases.
- ISA 450, ISA 560, ISA 570, ISA 580, ISA 700/705/706 are covered in completion/reporting.

## Question-Level Rules
- Every required question has a clear prompt, expected evidence count, and review status field.
- Every question has at least one citation (`ISA`, `ISQM`, `IFRS`, or `NIGERIA_REGULATION`).
- Industry templates include mandatory Nigeria legal references where applicable.

## Runtime Controls
- A procedure cannot be marked reviewed when mandatory questions are unanswered.
- A procedure cannot be marked reviewed when unresolved exceptions remain.
- Compliance exceptions are visible in export outputs.

## Certification Pack
- ISA/ISQM coverage matrix exports successfully.
- Compliance exceptions report exports successfully.
- Template application version log exports successfully.
- Reviewer override log exports successfully.
